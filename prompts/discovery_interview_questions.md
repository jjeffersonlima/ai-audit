# Prompt: Interview Question Generator

**When to use:** Preparing for stakeholder interviews or discovery calls.

**Prompt Template:**

```text
Generate 10 discovery questions for a [ROLE/TITLE] at a [COMPANY TYPE] business. The questions should:
- Use open-ended language to encourage detailed responses
- Uncover hidden inefficiencies in daily workflows
- Identify integration gaps between their tools
- Assess readiness for automation
- Reveal budget and timeline expectations

Format as a natural conversation flow, not an interrogation.
```
