# Prompt: Initial Business Context Gathering

**When to use:** At the start of your discovery call or immediately after.

**Prompt Template:**

```text
Act as an experienced business consultant conducting a discovery session. I'm auditing a [INDUSTRY TYPE] business with approximately [NUMBER] employees that uses [LIST PRIMARY TOOLS].

Before analyzing anything, ask me questions to understand:
1. Their primary business challenges
2. What processes consume the most time
3. Where errors or delays frequently occur
4. What tools don't communicate with each other
5. Their goals for the next 12 months

Ask one category at a time and wait for my response before continuing.
```
